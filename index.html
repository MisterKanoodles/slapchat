Your quick guide

Everything here is a demo, so you can try the flow without sending anything.

1
Take a snap

Tap “Turn on camera” and allow camera access, or choose “Use iPhone camera.” Tap the yellow button to capture. Your photo stays in this preview.

2
Look through Stories

Tap a friend’s circle. Swipe the row sideways to see more friends.

3
Install on iPhone

Open the live Slapchat website in Safari. Tap the Share button, scroll down, then tap “Add to Home Screen.”

4
Try chats

Tap Chats in the bottom bar to open the sample conversation list. Sending messages is not connected yet.
